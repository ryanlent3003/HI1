KEY FINDINGS SUMMARY
================================================================================

1. SNOWMELT TIMING:
   - Wet year (2019): Peak flows June 16 (DOY 167) for Dolores
   - Dry year (2014): Peak flows October 1 (DOY 274) for Dolores
   - Headwater shows earlier, sharper peaks (June 9, DOY 160 for 2019)

2. RESERVOIR EFFECTS:
   - Dolores: Attenuated peaks, buffered flows (regulated)
   - Headwater: Sharp peaks, natural variability (unregulated)
   - Wet/dry flow ratio: 4.5x vs 10.9x respectively

3. AGGREGATION PATTERNS:
   - Daily data shows high variability and noise
   - Weekly aggregation smooths short-term fluctuations
   - Monthly data clearly shows seasonal patterns

4. HYDROLOGIC IMPLICATIONS:
   - Regulation fundamentally alters natural flow regime
   - Snowmelt-dominated streams show predictable patterns
   - Timing differences critical for water management
